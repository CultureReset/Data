body: My family and I live right down the road from Asian Fusion and somehow never realized it until tonight, but we are so glad we finally tried it. This is, without exaggeration, the best Asian food we’ve had in years.

The Chef was incredibly welcoming and kind, the service was fast, and every dish we tried was absolutely phenomenal. A quick note for anyone sensitive to spice: even the “mild” lo mein and General’s chicken have a noticeable kick. We love spice, so it was perfect for us, but it’s worth mentioning.

We ordered the pork lo mein, and it was so good it nearly made my dad emotional with how flavorful and nostalgic it tasted. The pork itself was outstanding. Honestly, if you’re planning to get lo mein… order three. You’ll want more the moment you finish.

The fried rice was so delicious we were eating it completely plain—no soy sauce needed at all. The egg rolls were some of the best any of us have ever had, and my dad travels all over for work, so that says a lot. The General’s chicken was fantastic (again, spicy even on the mild level), and the orange chicken was a huge hit with the whole family.

Our only disappointment was that we didn’t have second and third stomachs to keep eating. We will absolutely be back!
