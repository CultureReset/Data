Clearly, most units here a PERMANENT and a few are “rough around the edges” as was our neighbors..lots of junk. Regardless of the location, it is a $50.00 park tops.  We paid just slightly more on the “Keys water front with a pool, hot tub cable TV, Wifi etc”.
