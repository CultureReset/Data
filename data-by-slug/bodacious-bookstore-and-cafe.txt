This little bookstore is fair enough, but I likely won’t go to the trouble of coming back. I’ve noticed a trend in the panhandle for book shops that have a small selection of specifically curated books to appeal to the reading masses (as well they should because that’s where the money to stay open lies) along with another thing or two to draw folks in, and that’s what the Bodacious Bookstore amounts to. Like many of these other small places, the number of hard covers and sprayed edges offered is proportionately highter than at the big box stores, and I love that. It makes the shelves absolutely beautiful.

Some of the less visited sections have to be left behind in these stores, though, and I get that. Don’t expect to find a huge reference section although they did have a decent “local/Florida” section. The poetry section was literally the two books in the included photo, and there were no “arts” or “religion” or “philosophy”  sections that I saw. Think more “books for vacation,” and you’ll have the vibe here.

The staff wasn’t rude, but they also weren’t exactly warm, and there were lots of them. Had I needed help, I wouldn’t have had any trouble finding it.

After I left, I thought about why I had no desire to go back, and I think it’s because overall it didn’t feel like a particularly warm or welcoming space. “Sterile” is the word that comes to mind for some reason. That crispness might appeal to a lot of people, though.

They did have a few fun bookish gifts, in particular the Book Wallet brand items and ceramic book planters or pencil holder type things.  Overall, though, not a huge inventory of non-book items.

The long and short of it is that if you’re in the area, do make a stop and support this independent book shop. I might or might not, but I won’t make a special trip over to Pensacola just to come here like I did the day I visited. I’ll stick to my more local stores instead.

body: ***Please note two things that influence this review: 1.  I did not try anything from the cafe, but it looked cute. 2.  I live in a tiny home, so each item in it must earn its keep, so I buy very few physical books unless they’re gifts for others. ***

This little bookstore is fair enough, but I likely won’t go to the trouble of coming back. I’ve noticed a trend in the panhandle for book shops that have a small selection of specifically curated books to appeal to the reading masses (as well they should because that’s where the money to stay open lies) along with another thing or two to draw folks in, and that’s what the Bodacious Bookstore amounts to. Like many of these other small places, the number of hard covers and sprayed edges offered is proportionately highter than at the big box stores, and I love that. It makes the shelves absolutely beautiful.

Some of the less visited sections have to be left behind in these stores, though, and I get that. Don’t expect to find a huge reference section although they did have a decent “local/Florida” section. The poetry section was literally the two books in the included photo, and there were no “arts” or “religion” or “philosophy”  sections that I saw. Think more “books for vacation,” and you’ll have the vibe here.

The staff wasn’t rude, but they also weren’t exactly warm, and there were lots of them. Had I needed help, I wouldn’t have had any trouble finding it.

After I left, I thought about why I had no desire to go back, and I think it’s because overall it didn’t feel like a particularly warm or welcoming space. “Sterile” is the word that comes to mind for some reason. That crispness might appeal to a lot of people, though.

They did have a few fun bookish gifts, in particular the Book Wallet brand items and ceramic book planters or pencil holder type things.  Overall, though, not a huge inventory of non-book items.

The long and short of it is that if you’re in the area, do make a stop and support this independent book shop. I might or might not, but I won’t make a special trip over to Pensacola just to come here like I did the day I visited. I’ll stick to my more local stores instead.
