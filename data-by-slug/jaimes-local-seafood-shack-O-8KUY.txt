body: Officially, I would rate this place as a 3.5. The owner of the food truck sources her seafood from the seafood shop next door. It is definitely excellent quality. The outdoor seating on the wharf is beautiful, especially on a clear day. The Alligator Bites were similar to a hush puppy, as one review stated, with a little spice along with a little bit of cheese. Like a mozzarella stick and an Alligator Bite fused together. The fried crawfish poboy would be good for someone who likes a lot of breading. The bread they use for sandwiches is excellent, very fluffy with that touch of butter and flavor. The fried grouper basket was apparently tasty, but unfortunately, she used grouper fingers instead of fillets. The lobster roll was good, with the lemon dressing, even including shrimp in there. However, I think it is more shrimp than lobster, which I would have wanted the latter to be the star of the show. Unfortunately, there is a giant markup on the food. Also, there was the odd occurrence that the owner charged for someone who wanted to add coleslaw, when the other member did not want the coleslaw that came with the fried grouper basket. It's delicious, but I would advise you to choose wisely and take complete responsibility for any questions you have about the menu and pricing.
