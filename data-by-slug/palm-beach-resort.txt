The C/D pool heater is working just fine.” While we appreciated the response, it was disappointing since the functioning heated pool became very crowded and the closer one was unusable.

One thing worth noting is the building layout — if someone is smoking on their balcony, the smell carries very strongly to neighboring units. It’s unavoidable due to how the building is shaped.

The elevators worked well. The fitness room closest to us was weight-focused; we didn’t explore the others, but I hope there are additional rooms with cardio equipment.

Overall, while there were positives, I would not pay the price we did for this same unit again. If we returned, it would be during warmer weather when a heated pool isn’t necessary, and I would likely opt for a privately owned unit instead.
