Once we got back, Captain Jeff cleaned and filleted all of our fish right there, and it turned into a whole production. Pelicans, herons, and seagulls were swooping in for scraps like it was their full-time job, while little crabs were down below hustling for leftovers. It was basically dinner and a show for everyone involved.

The boat and equipment were great, the company was even better, and the entire trip felt fun, easy, and memorable from start to finish.

If you’re thinking about booking, just do it! We would 100% go again and will probably still be talking about this trip for a while.
