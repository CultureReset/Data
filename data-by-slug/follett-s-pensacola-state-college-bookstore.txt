body: Follett’s Pensacola State College Bookstore is honestly a pretty decent place to get what you need for classes. They’ve got textbooks, rentals, supplies, and even some cool PSC merch. The workers there are usually really nice and helpful too—I’ve had a few times where they helped me find the exact book I needed when I couldn’t figure it out online. The Inclusive Access thing is also nice since it gives you digital materials cheaper and ready by the first day.

That said, it’s not perfect. The prices can be kinda high, especially for access codes and new books. I’ve noticed you can find some stuff cheaper online if you look around. Ordering online through them can also be a bit annoying—sometimes the shipping is slow or things get mixed up. During the first week of classes it gets super busy too, so be ready to wait in line for a while. Plus, the hours aren’t that great on Fridays or weekends, which kinda sucks if you work or have a busy schedule.

Overall though, it’s a good place to grab what you need fast. It’s convenient and reliable, just maybe not the cheapest option out there.
