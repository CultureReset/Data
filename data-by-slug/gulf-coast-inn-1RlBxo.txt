The location is great, the rooms are super clean, spacious and well appointed. Actually I was very surprised as to how well the Inn is kept. The grounds are in excellent condition. The pool is clean. It is a remarkable little boutique Inn / Motel that you're not expecting.

What sets this place truly apart from other accommodations is the staff.

Every person I met that works at Gulf Coast Inn from the grounds person to housekeeping had happy dispositions and asked all the time, "Can I do anything for you?" -  "Can I make your stay better?"
