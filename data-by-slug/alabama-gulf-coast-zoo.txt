The lemur encounter was disappointing though. $25/person, so it adds up. Now, the care taker was very nice and very informative and no qualms about him. Their enclosure was also very nice and clean. This new encounter you get some biscuits and tongs and feed them and can only pet their back. I understand there was some potential USDA ruling that changed how the encounters work. But going from holding and carrying the lemurs to only being able to touch their backs is a bit 🙃, but my daughter enjoyed it, so that's all that matters.

The new amur leopard was really cool! It was back in its cage and I crouched down to take a picture (since that was the only way to see it) and it went into attack mode. It crouched low, big eyed, crept towards me, and then pounced at the fence close by! I can only imagine how intimidating that would be in the wild.

Overall, we had a great time and will probably come back in a few years, but it just felt a bit... lacking.
