The pizza, however, was so-so. Excellent crust in the NY style pies (Sicilian would have been good had it not been burnt well beyond the char expected of the style) and one of the best sauces I've had anywhere. The toppings are good quality but not exceptional. The cheese is what will keep me from ordering here again. Mama Mia's could really have a great pie but the cheese was so bad. Flavorless and overly soft with zero bite or pull. It turned what could have been great pizza into pizza we'll never order again. Having been a chef for 20 years, I understand that the price of cheese is a real pain point for pizzarias but, I would have gladly paid a few more dollars per pie for quality cheese. Cheese is not the place to cut food cost; maybe get rid some of those low-margin toppings instead. It really is a shame because they seem to have the hard parts (crust and sauce) dialed in. Oh well. On to the next.
