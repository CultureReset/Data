Located right in the heart of downtown in the city’s historic first library building, it is so much more than just a place to pick up a map. It’s a goldmine for anyone looking to make the most of their vacation budget.

The Ultimate Savings Hub
If you want to save money, this should be your very first stop in town. The amount of discounts and coupons available is incredible.

The Coupon Sheet: They have an official coupon sheet (often featuring over 70 local deals!) that can save you hundreds of dollars on dining, shopping, and attractions like OWA or the Tanger Outlets.

Insider Info: The lady at the front desk was a fountain of knowledge. She didn't just give us brochures; she gave us the "local's guide" on where to eat and which attractions were currently offering the best value.

Freebies: To top it all off, she was kind enough to give us some free stickers! It was such a sweet, generous gesture that made the kids’ day and gave us a little memento of our visit.

Quality Souvenirs
Aside from the free info, they have a fantastic selection of t-shirts for sale. Unlike the generic shirts you see elsewhere, these are high-quality, beautifully designed, and really capture the spirit of Foley. They make for the perfect "non-tacky" souvenir to remember the trip by.

Stopping here first probably saved us enough money to pay for a whole extra dinner out. Between the warm hospitality, the wealth of local tips, and the cool gear, the Welcome Center is easily the most "valuable" five minutes you can spend in the city.
