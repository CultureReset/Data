As soon as we stepped in, we were greeted warmly by multiple employees, all with big smiles. The hospitality alone makes a great first impression.

The menu is short and simple, which I actually loved — it made choosing easy. I ordered the Cheeseburger along with a side of Frings (shoestring fries + onion rings) and their house-made ranch.

While we waited, one of the employees told us about a fun game featured in the town’s weekly newsletter they keep for customers. There’s a tiny hidden character in the ads, and if you find it, you can enter a monthly gift-card drawing. There’s also trivia. It was such a unique touch and made the wait genuinely fun.

Our food came out quickly, and everything was excellent. The burger was incredibly juicy without being greasy, packed with seasoning, and absolutely delicious. The fries and onion rings were great, too, and both their burger sauce and house-made ranch were chef’s kiss — fresh, flavorful, and clearly homemade.

Overall, Cheeseburger Randy’s is absolutely worth the drive. Great food, great people, and a great atmosphere. Highly recommend!
