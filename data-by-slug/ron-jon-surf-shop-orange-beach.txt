Located right on Perdido Beach Boulevard, the bright blue and sandy-toned building practically screams "beach vacation" before you even step through the front doors.
While it isn't the massive, multi-story mega-store like their famous flagship location in Cocoa Beach, this spot packs a massive punch for its size. It's the ultimate one-stop shop for anyone trying to embrace the coastal lifestyle, whether you are a local or just down here for the week.
As soon as you walk in, you're hit with that classic surf shop aroma—a nostalgic mix of coconut sunscreen, fresh apparel, and surfboard wax. The layout is clean and incredibly spacious, making it easy to browse without bumping elbows with other shoppers, even during peak summer tourist season.
They have a massive inventory that caters to absolutely everyone:
* **The Apparel:** Racks upon racks of high-quality swimwear, boardshorts, and sundresses from top brands like Billabong, Quiksilver, and Roxy.
* **The Famous Tees:** You can't leave without one. They have an endless selection of the classic, world-famous Ron Jon Surf Shop t-shirts and hoodies, complete with the "Orange Beach, AL" print.
* **The Gear:** If you're looking to actually hit the water, they have got you covered with a solid selection of bodyboards, skimboards, skateboards, sunglasses, and sandals.
The staff here is always super laid-back, friendly, and helpful, perfectly matching the easygoing vibe of the Alabama Gulf Coast. Prices are pretty standard for a major brand name surf retailer, but they consistently have a clearance section tucked away where you can find some absolute steals on apparel.
Whether you need a high-quality rash guard to protect yourself from the sun, forgot your flip-flops, or just want a cool souvenir sticker for your cooler, Ron Jon is absolutely worth dropping by. It’s a fun, vibrant shopping experience that immediately puts you in a vacation state of mind.
