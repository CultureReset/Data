Condo is within about 10 minutes of a Walmart and 5 minutes from a general store with all necessities.
Things to consider. There are no elevators so either way you will be walking a couple to 4 flights of steps. This ok for us but there is no physical impairment for us that makes this difficult. Also I don’t think they mention it, but there is only enough toilet paper to get you through the day. So you will need toilet paper, garbage bags, dish soap and any detergents needed.
The kitchen comes with all your basic needs.  Don’t forget a few essential seasonings such as salt and pepper. These are usually not here.
There are usually lots of people staying here that fish because of the location to the lagoon and the inlet just down the street.  They have a setup to clean fish on the pier which is very nifty to have.
Beach access is maybe a hundred yard walk from the condo parking lot to where you’d place your chair in the sand.
Pools are always clean, not heated but always very clear.
So overall we have stayed here twice and will continue to stay here as we enjoy the location, service, and people we encounter here.
