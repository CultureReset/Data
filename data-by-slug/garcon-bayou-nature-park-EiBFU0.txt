body: Dogs are not allowed which was disappointing, but I understand that it is primarily a children’s park. We visited when it was wet, so I’m not sure how it is when it’s dry, but you need to be very careful on the boardwalk. To say the wood was slippery is an understatement. It’s fairly dangerous, especially for children, elderly, or disabled people. And perhaps we went at a bad time, but it was in need of maintenance. Not only is it deteriorating and layered with natural grime/muck, shrubs and thorny vines trespass the walkway.

It is very small. Imagine a letter T design where you walk up the center (about 200 feet, and then there is about 150 feet of boardwalk to the left and right. Takes about 2 minutes to cover the entire trail if you weren’t to stop and take in the surroundings. Speaking of, there is actually a vast wetlands (?) behind it, but the trees and shrubs are overgrowing and block most of the view of that and the cool moss ponds.

If this park were to get a massive donation one day, or funds allocated from the county, and if the land allowed, they could make an amazing long and winding path over the large field you see in the photo. Could be really amazing.

Unless you are there with your kids for the playground, I wouldn’t waste your time unless you are already driving by.
