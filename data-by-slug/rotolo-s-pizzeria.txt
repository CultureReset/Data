On the positive side, the service was excellent—attentive without being overbearing, and there was no wait to be seated, which is always welcome in this area! The atmosphere was also enjoyable: not too loud, with TVs on and low background music that didn’t interfere with conversation. There’s a small bar area that seems ideal for solo diners or couples, and they offer several draft options, which is great!

We started with appetizers, which were solid overall. The spinach dip stood out, though it was served with baked pizza crust instead of the usual chips or bread. It was an interesting choice—I'm just not convinced it was the best pairing.

As for the pizza, it was underwhelming. If you’re expecting New York, New Haven, or Neapolitan-style pizza, you’ll likely be disappointed. It seems like Detroit-style might be their strong suit. The pizza we had came out with a top that was very well done—bordering on burned—while the crust underneath was undercooked and spongy. It made for an odd texture contrast, and I wasn’t sure what kind of oven or method they were using.

Overall, while the service and atmosphere were great, the pizza itself didn’t quite hit the mark. Admittedly I'm a pizza snob and if the crust doesn't cut it, it's just cheese on bread to me. I’m not sure I’d go out of my way to return, but I wouldn’t rule it out entirely—mainly because of how well we were treated.

body: I stopped by this pizza spot recently and had a bit of a mixed experience. On the positive side, the service was excellent—attentive without being overbearing, and there was no wait to be seated, which is always welcome in this area! The atmosphere was also enjoyable: not too loud, with TVs on and low background music that didn’t interfere with conversation. There’s a small bar area that seems ideal for solo diners or couples, and they offer several draft options, which is great!

We started with appetizers, which were solid overall. The spinach dip stood out, though it was served with baked pizza crust instead of the usual chips or bread. It was an interesting choice—I'm just not convinced it was the best pairing.

As for the pizza, it was underwhelming. If you’re expecting New York, New Haven, or Neapolitan-style pizza, you’ll likely be disappointed. It seems like Detroit-style might be their strong suit. The pizza we had came out with a top that was very well done—bordering on burned—while the crust underneath was undercooked and spongy. It made for an odd texture contrast, and I wasn’t sure what kind of oven or method they were using.

Overall, while the service and atmosphere were great, the pizza itself didn’t quite hit the mark. Admittedly I'm a pizza snob and if the crust doesn't cut it, it's just cheese on bread to me. I’m not sure I’d go out of my way to return, but I wouldn’t rule it out entirely—mainly because of how well we were treated.
