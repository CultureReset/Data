body: Big Lagoon State Park is one of my favorite places in the Pensacola area, and I’m out here at least once or twice a week. It’s a great spot whether you want to launch a kayak, walk the trails, or just enjoy some time outdoors.

The park is very well maintained and the staff at the entrance gate are always friendly and welcoming. It’s easy to get in and out of, which makes it perfect for quick visits or spending the whole afternoon exploring.

One of the highlights is the observation tower where you can look out across the water and actually see the Gulf over at Johnson Beach. It’s also a fantastic place for sunsets and birdwatching, and I regularly see deer when I’m walking the trails.

They also offer camping and have kayak and paddleboard rentals available, which makes it a great place for visitors as well as locals.

A few of the boardwalks still show some hurricane damage and look like they’re waiting to be replaced, but overall the park is in great condition.

In my opinion, this is probably the best state park in this part of Northwest Florida.
