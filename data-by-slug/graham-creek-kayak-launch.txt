body: Update:  we went back yesterday, and Saw 3 juvenile dolphins frolicking in the channel, just before the " no wake" bouy. They swam upstream towards the launch, and we saw them again on our return. Not quick enough with the camera

Easy land access, with signage to the launch. The launch itself is difficult to use. There is a very steep angle to the water. There is an adjacent flat platform but it sits about a foot above the water level, and floats. It is difficult to get into the kayak if you are by yourself if someone were holding it it would probably be easier. It would also be beneficial if they would post a map showing the lay of the bayou. I used Google Maps so I had no problems but it would be nice to see the layout before launching. This is a Tidewater Bayou so current depends on whether tide is rising or falling or at full height or low height. there was no current when I went out. I went to the left which is south east I believe about 45 minutes of paddling to Wolf bay. The very pleasant paddle, with lots of nature scenery some fish jumping, and occasional crane. At the bay itself the houses with boat houses and raised boats was very interesting. Overall a very enjoyable paddle.
