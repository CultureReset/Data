The aisles are very narrow in spots… I did see someone with a shopping cart, but maybe it was for show. There are also lots of breakable glass items, so if we spent too much time in there with small kids, my anxiety level would definitely creep up. Okay for a quick pop-in, though!
The checkout process is kinda madness because there’s no obvious line, but my complaint on  that is minimal because I was able to hop in behind a group of old ladies and not wait too long. Others that came after might feel differently.
I think this is definitely worth a drop in if you’re in Gulf Shores. I mean, you gotta go to a Shark Shop when you’re in a beach town!
