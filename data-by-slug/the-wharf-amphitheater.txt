body: This could be a great amphitheater.

There is literally no one in charge of parking.

You will see several lots with plenty of spaces, but you will not be able to enter.
There is pre-paid parking, but it, too, is iffy as to if you will actually be able to park there.

Handicapped parking is awkwardly located and extremely limited. If you are handicapped, make other arrangements.

We waited 1.5hrs after our event and traffic did not move at all.

There are officers present, but they are not there to direct traffic. No one is.

If you attend, either Uber in & out or go with the knowledge you will spend an extra 2-3hrs JUST on parking.

The seats are aluminum bleachers. They have a small back on them in some sections, but others have no backs at all.

The staff is mostly friendly & helpful WITH A FEW EXCEPTIONS. I would advise not engaging if you can help it.
