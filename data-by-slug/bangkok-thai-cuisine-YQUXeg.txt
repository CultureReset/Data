body: Bangkok Thai: A Disaster from Start to Finish
From the moment I sat down, I knew I had made a mistake. The chairs? Still wrapped in plastic—but not in a “fresh and clean” way. The plastic is shredded and literally rips into your thighs. It’s like an involuntary exfoliation session, but only if you enjoy suffering.

The service? Barely existent. And if you need proof of how little they care about ambiance, just check the Lagniappe newspaper at the front—from 2022. Apparently, that’s the last time anyone here bothered to update… well, anything.

Now, the food. Oh. My. God.

Crunchy Chicken? Completely inedible. And I eat everything. It was drowning in oil, barely had any actual chicken, and the sauce was so painfully sweet it might as well have been dessert.

Seaweed Salad? Warm. Yes, warm. And it tasted like it had been sitting out for weeks.

Spring Rolls? Thin, flavorless, and sad. Two measly shrimp thrown in as if that would help.

Tom Kha Gai Soup? Barely lukewarm when it arrived, plopped in front of me without even a plate underneath. The waitress disappeared, so I had to bring it to her at the checkout stand just to ask for it to be reheated. The broth was decent, but at that point, I was too annoyed to care.

Tom Yum Soup with Tofu? The tofu was so dry and tough, it was borderline inedible.

I walked in hungry, and I left with nothing but regret. DO NOT waste your time or your money here.
