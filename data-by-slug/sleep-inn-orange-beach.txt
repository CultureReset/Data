The location was fantastic with wonderful, easy access to the beach. Watching the sunrise and sunset right from the property was truly the highlight of the trip.

The room itself was just okay. It definitely needs a deep clean. The lamps had what looked like mold or dirt rings, and I wish I had checked more carefully before placing my clothes on the furniture—everything picked up a layer of dust and dirt. The bathroom shower also had an odd-looking patch job. The drapes had holes that let in sunlight, which made napping difficult and made the room feel a bit worn down.

On the positive side, the bed and sheets were clean, and the temperature was easy to keep comfortable switching between heat and air.

Housekeeping was a mixed experience. I had to request clean towels daily, even after being told they would be switched out. I didn’t expect full service during the holiday, but it would have been nice to at least have towels refreshed, trash emptied, and basic items like soap and tissue replenished.

The staff, however, were all very friendly and hospitable. Breakfast was a great start to the day, and the common areas were clean and well maintained.

Overall, it was a fantastic holiday with an unbeatable location—just go in knowing that the rooms could use some attention and a deeper clean. Cannot beat the price!
