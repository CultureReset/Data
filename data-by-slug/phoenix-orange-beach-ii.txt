Pros:

Great views and beach. Unit has expansive ocean views and you can see the sunset and far down the beach if you look out to the west.

Unit was clean though lots of paint damage on the walls mostly from people rubbing luggage against it I think.

Communication before and during stay with Brett Robinson was excellent and responsive.

Pools are nice but outside pool is not heated and is very cold this time of year.

I read criticism of elevator wait times at other Phoenix properties but had no issues here.

Security was frequently around and staff was all friendly.

Cons:

Bedroom and provided kitchen items in the unit are mostly terrible. The beds were hard and uncomfortable. The master had one small set of drawers that would fall out if u opened them, we ended up putting clothes in the bathroom cabinets. The second bedroom had a cheap set of clear plastic drawers in them like what u would get at Walmart. The kitchenware was super basic and cheap, it would be difficult to do any real cooking though the coffee maker did both kcups and pots which was the bright spot. Overall these aspects are disappointing in units that sell in the neighborhood of 1.5 mil.

Though they provided lots of bath towels, we were told the towels were only for in unit use. Though we could get away with taking the towels to the pool, we were told specifically the towels could not be used on the beach. This is disappointing when you are renting a unit that's main selling point is being on the beach. Additionally, outside of towels and bath soaps, they provide one thin roll of toilet paper in each bathroom, one extra trash bag for the whole unit,   a sink sponge and one pouch of dishwasher detergent with no dish soap, washing machine pods or any other niceties. I know this is somewhat par for the course for corporately managed rentals, but still disappointing. There is definitely a huge gap in what u get from caring Air BnB owners and corporate management companies in this regard so more of an industry critism than Brett Robinson specific.

Oddly, the bedrooms which were both at the back side of the unit had windows to the common hallway with plantation shutters over them. The shutters did not fully block the light which made it difficult to sleep and every time you woke up you thought the sun was out even if it was the middle of the night. I am guessing these windows are required by building code but should have black out curtains.

There are no umbrellas around the outdoor pool with the loungers which we thought was odd. We always bring small single person umbrellas with us that clamp on to the chairs for shade as our family has a history of skin cancer. When we put them up, security came by and told us they don't allow umbrellas because they are a liability and could blow into someone (he couldn't really explain the details - it was a flat calm day). This is not listed anywhere I ever saw and there is no signage. It is the first trip I have ever gone on that doesn't allow umbrellas and is a reason I wouldn't stay at the property again. Note that the neighboring property has umbrellas by the pool, and management even advertises you can rent umbrellas on the beach.

This last point isn't the properties fault, but the location and I think much of Orange Beach in general is not walkable to shops and restaurants. We had to drive to cross the street to get ice cream (literally 500 feet away) because it would have been almost a mile walk to get to a marked crosswalk. The other option would have been running across a busy 5 lane road with our kids.

In conclusion, the deal breakers on returning for us are the lack of pool shade, hard beds and inability to get the bedrooms dark enough due to the windows to the hallway. If these were remedied we would definitely consider returning.
