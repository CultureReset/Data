The flavors in the crab spread were excellent and brought out the crab. Everything was very fresh and it was clear that the ingredients were high quality.

The water served to us was filtered water, not tap water. It's all too common to be served tap water and the difference is stark.

For the entree I had the Gulf Grouper with the pineapple-mango salsa. It was served with vegetables and coconut rice. The fish was well-seasoned and all the flavors of the dish worked great together. I loved the salsa, despite being a sweet pineapple-mango salsa, it still had enough heat as any salsa should.

Overall I'd highly recommend Tide and Table if you're looking for an amazing dinning experience and exceptional quality food. There really is no other venue in the immediate vicinity of the Gulf Shores area that matches this. To get a similar experience you'd need to visit the Perch at the Lodge or Voyagers at the Peridido.

Also, huge shout out to the staff, which provided exceptional service, something I always want went visiting a restaurant like this.
